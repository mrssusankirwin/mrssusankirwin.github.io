About Me

I'm Susan Kirwin, a self-taught artist living and painting in Howth, Co. Dublin, where I look after my husband, five boys and two dogs. My background was engineering and maths, and I now work in education. Painting is my creative release, and gives me quiet space to myself, despite the background chaos.

I work mostly in oils on canvas, watercolour, pen & ink, acrylic, pastels and charcoal, and I paint any subject. I'm always happy to take on the challenge a commission brings.

I exhibit locally, in Dublin city centre and North Dublin, and in OffGrid Coffee in Howth.
